# Guest welcome: next steps

Open `index.html` in a browser. Flags: `?dark` for the dark theme, `?clean` to
hide the blue research notes.

The mockup is the live guest page as it ships today, plus four additions taken
from the research below. Nothing in `src/` was changed.

## The four additions, and why

1. **Get directions on the door sign.** A guest deciding to walk in needs the
   way there next to "Open until 6 PM". It goes on the sign that already holds
   the shop's status, so it adds no new element.
2. **The brand line.** The stamp's own words, "Take that much needed break",
   set once at full size between the menu and the tour. It sits on the plain
   cream ground, not a new panel, so the page keeps its rhythm. The one orange
   word is the page's accent, used the way the hero uses it.
3. **The story.** Founding year large on the left, three short lines on the
   right, in the hero's own 5:7 split. Every line is a blank for the shop.
4. **Address and directions in the footer.** The footer already has hours and
   Instagram; this completes "Find us".

## Blanks the shop has to fill

- Founding year (`20__`)
- Three story lines and a photo of the people behind the counter
- Street address (comes from Settings once an admin fills it in)
- The shop's exact map pin. `8.15° N, 125.13° E` is Malaybalay's city centre,
  shown dashed until the real pin replaces it.
- The directions link searches Google Maps for "Hiatus Coffee Malaybalay"; a
  link to the shop's own Maps listing would be more reliable.
