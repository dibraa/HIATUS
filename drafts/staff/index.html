The flow every direction serves — one screen, one loop per order, payment folded into hand-over (today it lives on a separate POS tab):

1 · NewOrder arrives: highlighted, counted in the top bar (and a chime, in the real build).
2 · StartOne big button. Late orders say "Late" / "Over 12m" — never colour alone.
3 · ReadyMark ready; the customer is notified.
4 · Hand overCustomer arrives. If unpaid, the payment panel opens right there: quick cash amounts + change, or e-wallet.
5 · DonePaid and handed over in one tap. Sold-out and shift stay one tap away in the top bar.
